# Human Study Instruction Sheet

Listen with headphones in a quiet environment. Review each anonymized video-
music pair once in its entirety, then assign the requested ratings independently.
Use the full 1-5 scale. Do not infer model identity from ordering; samples should
be randomized and model names hidden.

Participation, consent, privacy, compensation, and ethics-review language must
be supplied by the authors according to their institution and study protocol.
This template is not an ethics approval document.
